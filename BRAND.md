# Spider OS Brand System

## Core identity

**Name:** Spider OS  
**Tagline:** **YOUR LIFE. ONE WEB.**  
**AI:** Webbie

Spider OS represents the user's whole life. Broken Sorrow and every other project live inside the system as connected Anchors; none of them replaces the master identity.

## Master emblem

The official emblem is the user's original angular spider mark, previously used for their tattoo studio. The system version is crimson and clean enough for icons; the distressed version is reserved for large artwork and key moments.

- Canonical crimson: `#DC143C`
- Never round or soften the silhouette.
- Never place the mark inside a generic circle or app-store-style squircle unless a platform forces an icon mask.
- Small icons use the clean mark.
- Boot, wallpaper, lock-screen, installation, and campaign artwork may use the distressed mark.

## Color system

| Role | Name | Value |

| raised surfaces | Graphite | `#1A1C20` |

Crimson signals identity, focus, activation, and meaningful warnings. It is not decorative confetti. Ordinary interface text is bone rather than sterile white.

## Typography

The **SPIDER OS** display wordmark uses a custom **architectural Gothic** construction: pointed arches, narrow verticals, structural cuts, and restrained tattoo-lineage details. It must remain readable at a glance.

Interface text uses a clean modern sans-serif. Gothic display lettering is reserved for branding, title moments, boot/splash screens, installer screens, and selected section dividers. It is not used for body copy, task lists, settings, or data-heavy screens.

## Texture

Distressed texture appears in **artwork and key moments only**:

- boot and shutdown sequences
- lock screen / login artwork
- major wallpapers
- installer and recovery imagery
- release/promotional artwork

Daily controls, panels, menus, fields, and text remain clean.

## Motion: Web Assembly

The signature startup sequence is **Web Assembly**.

1. Black screen with near-invisible graphite depth.
2. Thin crimson web strands grow inward from the edges toward a central node.
3. Additional strands connect, implying a network rather than a literal circular spider web.
4. The crimson spider emblem resolves from the junctions.
5. The architectural Gothic **SPIDER OS** wordmark appears.
6. **YOUR LIFE. ONE WEB.** fades in beneath it.
7. One restrained crimson pulse travels back outward through the web.
8. The web dissolves into the desktop.

Target length: roughly 2.0–2.6 seconds on cold startup. A reduced-motion mode uses a simple emblem/wordmark fade with no strand movement.

## Wallpaper families

Spider OS ships four coordinated families.

### 1. The Web — default
Near-black and graphite architecture with subtle, asymmetric thread geometry. The web should feel like connected systems, not Halloween decoration.

### 2. The Emblem
A large distressed crimson spider partly swallowed by black space. High impact, minimal clutter.

### 3. Bone & Crimson
Bone architectural planes, dark graphite structure, and controlled crimson fractures/threads. This is the lightest visual family without becoming a light theme.

### 4. Anchors
Dark web nodes representing major parts of life. Nodes remain abstract enough that new Anchors can be added without redesigning the entire wallpaper.

## Sound identity

The sound language is **subtle/dark with restrained industrial weight**. Sounds are short, low-fatigue, and usable every day.

- **Boot:** low sub swell, faint metal resonance, single restrained impact.
- **Unlock / ready:** short tension-release sound, like a tightened string settling.
- **Notification:** muted metal tap with a very short dark tail.
- **Warning:** low double pulse with a dry industrial edge; urgent without being shrill.
- **Webbie ready:** tiny reversed harmonic / bowed-metal-like shimmer.
- **Shutdown:** the boot language collapses backward into silence.

No bright chimes, arcade bleeps, faux-hacker beeps, or constant cinematic noise. Silence is part of the sound system.

## Webbie voice

Webbie is direct, highly capable, grounded, and dryly funny in ordinary use. Its humor is brief and observational, never a substitute for the answer. It becomes plainly caring and drops sarcasm around grief, crisis, safety, clinical risk, trauma, or other vulnerable situations.

It should:

- answer directly before adding commentary;
- use structured information when that improves clarity;
- remember boundaries between Anchors;
- avoid generic motivational filler;
- admit uncertainty rather than improvise facts;
- never claim a proposed action has already occurred;
- require approval for consequential changes.



## Webbie presence

Webbie is the always-active resident intelligence of Spider OS. Her visual identity is the crimson spider/web emblem, never a human face. She appears as both a taskbar presence and a floating edge orb that hides during full-screen work.

- Wake phrases: **Hey Webbie**, **Webbie**, **Hey Web**, and **Web**.
- Default address: **Cory**.
- Studio/Music address: **Justin**.
- Kali Bay / Security Lab address: **Spider**.
- Voice: female, natural, Australian accent; controlled rather than exaggerated.
- Proactivity: very high. Webbie uses a short dark-industrial attention cue before proactive speech.
- Screen awareness: continuously available, locally processed by default, with a visible crimson privacy indicator and per-app exclusions. Frames are not archived by default.
- Memory: Webbie may learn routines automatically, but learned memories must be inspectable, editable, forgettable, and attributable to their source.
