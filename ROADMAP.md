# Spider OS roadmap

Spider OS is the operating system. **The Web** is its desktop. **Webbie** is the resident intelligence. **Anchors** organize durable areas of life and **Threads** carry work across them.

## 0.5 Knowledge foundation — completed

- Always-on resident Webbie coordinator
- Continuous, provenance-tagged Personal Knowledge Web
- Versioned preload of approved user/project knowledge
- Graduated authority model
- Local-first AI model support
- Screen-awareness policy and visible-presence contract
- Web Assembly branding, sound identity, and wallpaper families
- Aurora/Universal Blue bootc distribution scaffold
- Isolated Kali environment

## 0.6 The Web desktop — completed

- Canonical desktop name: **The Web**
- Replace legacy Life Space language with **Anchors**
- Keep tasks/projects/ideas/events/research as **Threads**
- Persistent animated Webbie presence in the shell
- Webbie status: listening, thinking, researching, speaking, warning, offline
- Login and screen-wake greeting events
- Lock-screen Web with a restricted action surface
- Local screen awareness with a persistent crimson indicator
- Notification center for deadlines, important changes, and proactive findings
- “Webbie Found Something” research feed
- Presence modes: Home, Studio, School, Work, Driving, Kali Bay, Focus, Sleep

- Facts/observations/inferences separated in the Personal Knowledge Web — **implemented**
- Ephemeral screen-context intake with sensitive-window exclusion — **implemented**
- Due/overdue proactive alert engine and Webbie Found Something feed — **implemented**
- Freshness and contradiction analysis — next
- Calendar read/write with availability checks
- Appointment preparation and scheduling approval flow
- Deadline extraction from school/work material
- Daily login brief, due-soon alerts, and meaningful-change alerts
- Email/message drafting and permissioned sending adapters
- Routines and recurring automations
